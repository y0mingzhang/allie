Search is one family. Others recalibrate the model, infer the player’s style, consult past games, or reconcile disagreeing predictions. Each needs a reason to know something the first prediction missed.

Scope: predict what a human will play, with the neural checkpoint held fixed. Small calibrators or routers may still be fitted on development games. Success means lower human next-move cross-entropy (CE), not simply stronger chess.
The key constraint

Imagining more futures does not automatically add information.

Suppose the model gives 40% to a bishop move. After that move, its opponent-reply probabilities sum to 100%. Multiplying by those reply probabilities and adding them back still gives 40%.

Σ replies P(move, reply) = P(move) × 1

For an exact autoregressive model, marginalizing its own continuations leaves its next-move distribution unchanged. Sampled rollouts add noise unless they estimate some additional quantity.

Useful algorithms therefore correct an approximation error, use extra evidence, or add a behavioral assumption—for example, that the player notices a tactical threat. That assumption must help predict humans, including their mistakes.

The backup sets the behavioral assumption.

Prior-weighted average: continue with human-like move probabilities.

Maximum / minimax: assume stronger best-response choices.

Soft Bellman: interpolate between those behaviors through a temperature.

Vτ(s) = τ log Σₐ p(a|s) exp(q(a)/τ)

Large τ approaches the prior-weighted mean; small τ approaches the maximum. Values change sign at every edge because the player to move changes. The WDL head supplies V = P(win) − P(loss), and rules supply terminal values.

The output decides how much to trust it.

Search value is evidence, not a command to pick the best move. Regularization keeps the human prior influential.

Exponential tilt: π ∝ p · exp(Q/λ)
Reverse KL: π = λp / (ν − Q)

ν normalizes the reverse-KL distribution. Reverse KL penalizes removing prior mass and more gently protects tails from value errors. Either objective still needs empirical calibration.

Released Allie uses reverse-KL output. The current stack uses calibrated exponential components, mixes their strengths, then applies temperature. That is separate from the internal soft backup.

Where released and repaired Allie fit on this map

Released adaptive Allie couples predicted-time budgets with PUCT and reverse-KL output. Our reference adapter uses N = clip(round(50 × predicted_seconds / 4.64001), 0, 200). It changes both the amount of search and regularization strength.

The repaired fixed baseline uses 50 simulations, fixes the zero-visit first choice to follow the prior, and preserves a previously expanded subtree at the depth boundary. These practical repairs are distinct from changing the output objective.

The audit also separates the paper’s exponential-tilt objective from the released reverse-KL formula, and the released cN/(K+N) output coefficient from the reference c√N/(K+N) coefficient.
